Questions and Answers

About Mary Lou Bates, her writing and her Antarctic memoir.

Who is Mary Lou Bates?

Mary Lou Bates is an author and rose enthusiast from Tennessee. She has published articles on rose culture in the New Zealand Rose Annual, the American Rose Annual and American Rose magazine, served as editor of KATnips (the Tenarky District Newsletter for Kentucky, Arkansas and Tennessee) and has written extensively for the Tennessee Rose Bud magazine.

What is she writing now?

A memoir about the time she and her husband Michael spent in Antarctica. Michael spent approximately 8 years in the U.S. Antarctic Research Program and almost 5 years on the ice, and Mary Lou spent 4 seasons in Antarctica following in his footsteps. The book also touches on New Zealand and Australia. Its byline: he promised her a rose garden if she would follow him to the ice. See the Antarctic Timeline.

When does the book come out?

Mary Lou is still writing it, so there is no release date yet. Sign up for book news on the home page and she will write when there is news.

How do I get news about the book?

Leave your first name and email in the Book News form on the home page of maryloubates.com. Mary Lou will write when there is news about her Antarctic memoir: a sneak peek, the cover, and the day you can buy it. Only a few emails, and you can unsubscribe any time.

Where can I read her writing?

Two of her rose articles are on this site: Mam-Maw Sarah's Yellow Roses (2009 American Rose Annual) and Roses Take You Away Down Memory Lane (2015 American Rose Annual). She also posts on Substack.

What is her Substack?

Mary Lou posts on Substack at https://marybatesroses.substack.com (@marybatesroses).

How do I contact Mary Lou?

Use the Get in Touch form at the bottom of the home page of maryloubates.com. You can also follow her Facebook author page or join her Facebook reader group.

Which writing groups is she part of?

Mary Lou is a member of Pitch to Published and Writing Away Refuge.
